# C012 — Source and comparison audit

The determinant, minor, local Smith and shared-background arguments in
this batch are written here with elementary proofs. They import no new
analytic or formal theorem. Novelty is unassessed.

Gabrić's [Words that almost commute](https://arxiv.org/html/2110.01120v2)
was inspected, including its definitions, the Fine–Wilf statement and
the two-mismatch classification. Rotation distance is an established
combinatorial object. Those results do not supply a denominator estimate
for arbitrary mismatch counts. This batch uses its own explicit marker
family and counting proof; it does not claim that small mismatch count
implies a bounded number of independent arithmetic terms.

The existing affine word convention and common-denominator identity are
recorded in [C004](../C004/ROTATION_LATTICE.md). The proposed application
of S-unit estimates must address the varying shared-arc intercepts;
[C003](../C003/REPORT.md)'s fixed-group reasoning cannot simply be
reused with those numbers treated as fixed coefficients. The obstruction
proved here keeps its bounded-arity and bounded-exponent hypotheses.
It is not a theorem excluding every use of S-unit methods.

The earlier logarithm and cycle-record inputs remain documented in
[C011's source audit](../C011/LITERATURE.md). No new analytic consequence
is claimed in C012. In particular, this batch neither improves a
universal cycle record nor excludes a new class of integer cycles.
Its all-size contribution is an exact obstruction to the proposed
elimination and compression methods. The original cycle goal is open.

## Leads screened without importing a theorem

- Simons's [Exotic Collatz cycles](https://www.impan.pl/shop/publication/transaction/download/product/82029)
  concerns a different multiplier domain, p>=5. It is not an imported
  theorem about the present multiplier3 denominator problem. References
  it makes to other papers were not substituted for reading those papers.
- Fernández and Ibáñez's [Christoffel words as extremal structures in
  Collatz dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24844) was screened at
  abstract level. Its extremal claim has not been adopted or verified
  here. Existing mechanical-word results remain the baseline.
- An attempted journal PDF retrieval returned an access error. No claim
  depends on the inaccessible text. Search snippets, forum claims and
  repository assertions found in the broader search were not premises.
