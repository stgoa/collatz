# C004 — What all rotations and cycle geometry actually force

**2026-10-19. Progress: an intrinsic classification and two exact
obstructions. The all-cycle headline remains active and open.**

C003 left a structural coverage gap: arbitrary cycles need not consist
of repeated blocks. This batch tests a mechanism on unrestricted words:
combine the divisibility conditions at every cyclic rotation with the
sizes of the resulting points. It yields a precise geometric criterion,
while rejecting independence of those congruences and a proposed
universal close-pair bound. No novelty or formal-verification claim is made.

## 1. A cycle inside one factor-two interval is exactly mechanical

Let a primitive positive rational cycle have m distinct odd states,
smallest L, largest H, and total valuation A. Then

```
H < 2L  iff its valuation word is a rotation of
a_j=floor((j+1)A/m)-floor(jA/m).
```

These are precisely the mechanical words already studied in R71.
For a narrow cycle with m>1, the theorem forces gcd(A,m)=1. Its reduced denominator is
exactly `D=2^A-3^m`, with no numerator cancellation. Fixed points are
included separately at m=1. Therefore any hypothetical nontrivial
positive integer Collatz cycle must have **H>2L**. Equality is impossible
because all reduced numerators are odd.

The [proof](ROTATION_LATTICE.md) uses only order and the existing exact
mechanical denominator theorem. Sort the states in `[L,2L)`. Their
values `3x+1` also span a ratio less than two. The exact division powers
therefore have two adjacent exponents, separated by one threshold in
sorted order. The successor permutation is a cyclic shift of ranks.
Following that shift gives the displayed floor-difference word.
Conversely, the mechanical phase coefficients are all less than twice
their minimum, term by term.

This gives an exact spectrum law across the generalized family. At a
fixed positive odd d, primitive raw cycles with H<2L are indexed by
coprime pairs `(A,m)`, `A>=m>=1`, satisfying

```
0 < 2^A-3^m divides d.
```

There is one cycle up to rotation for each such pair, obtained by
scaling its mechanical coefficients by `d/(2^A-3^m)`. This classification
does not claim an exhaustive solution of that exponential equation for
every fixed d. At d=1 only `(A,m)=(2,1)` remains.

**Scope of the gain:** the hypothesis is geometric and can be tested
on any cycle. The excluded word class itself is the mechanical class
already handled by R71. This is not a new general cycle-count record,
nor an exclusion of the wider cycles left by the theorem.

## 2. All rotations have one divisibility obstruction

For any positive valuation word with `D=2^A-3^m>0`, let `C_i` be its
rotated coefficients and `g=gcd(C_0,D)`. The entire cyclic system is

```
2^a_i C_(i+1)-3C_i=D.
```

Its cyclic integer matrix has Smith form `diag(1,...,1,D)`. Appending
the constant column gives Smith form `diag(1,...,1,g)`. The complete
integer kernel is generated by

```
(C_0/g,...,C_(m-1)/g,D/g).
```

Thus making all rotated states integral supplies exactly the original
denominator-divisibility condition. These congruences cannot be treated
as independent filters or independent random events. Positive odd
scaling is required for an actual raw odd-only cycle; the integer kernel
also contains points outside that domain.

There is a concrete cut version. For a one-letter cut at valuation a_i,
with its complementary word and commutator K_i,

```
gcd(D,K_i)=g*gcd(D/g,2^a_i-3).
```

At a_i=1 or 2 this is exactly g. Every hypothetical nontrivial d=1
integer cycle has a valuation-1 arrow, so one such cut already records
the full gcd. This obstruction concerns combining bare divisibilities;
it leaves open useful size estimates on the cut coefficients.

## 3. A universal close-pair bound fails, even at arbitrary heights

For rational cycle states with common reduced denominator d, the odd
lattice gives `d>=2/sigma`, where sigma is the smallest distinct-pair
distance. The proposed shortcut was to prove sigma<2 universally.
The actual d5 cycle

```
19 -> 31 -> 49 -> 19
```

normalizes to states `19/5,31/5,49/5` with sigma=12/5. It refutes the
proposal. The actual-cycle sample has 28 distinct normalized failures,
appearing in 183 scaled source instances.

The [stronger obstruction](SIZE_PROBE.md) proves that for every G>0
there is a primitive rational cycle with **every pair more than G
apart** and minimum greater than mG. These examples can still have
H<2L. Mechanical phase increments are single positive 2/3 monomials;
choosing coprime upper approximations `A-m log_2(3)->0+` makes their
normalized gaps arbitrarily large. The proof of these approximations
is elementary and retains the sign. Denominators vary with the examples;
no fixed-d assertion or arbitrary rescaling of the d1 map is used.

For example, the d13 mechanical cycle has ordered numerators
`319,367,421,485,557`, minimum gap 48/13 and ratio 557/319<2.
The exact `(A,m)=(485,306)` example has every gap greater than 100.

## 5. Decision and remaining programme

The all-rotation mechanism produces a real classification in one
geometric range. Its two simplest proposed extensions fail: the
congruences are dependent, and absolute proximity has no universal
bound. The next mechanism must retain information lost by those two
reductions, such as the order of states across different dyadic ranges
and how that order constrains cancellation. Another assumption that
all cycles fit inside a fixed range is not supplied here.

The side lane's new bounded-discrepancy proposal is assessed in
[SIDE_REVIEW.md](SIDE_REVIEW.md). Its necessary estimate does not by
itself bound discrepancy independently of period and minimum. Proving
exclusion for every finite discrepancy ceiling would recover the full
cycle headline, so that quantified proposal is not a smaller completion
criterion. The reported Lean checks of C001/C002 are useful validation,
not a formal verification of C004.
