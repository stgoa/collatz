# C008 primary-source scope

No novelty is asserted for the elementary cyclic-group or relation-lattice
argument. Its input `gcd(A,m)=1` is already in C005; C008 identifies its
denominator consequence and the exact sorted placement of residues.
The Hall statements and counterexamples are written and independently
checked within this repository, not claimed as new published mathematics.

## Sources checked in this batch

- Halbeisen and Hungerbühler, [Optimal bounds for the length of rational
  Collatz cycles](https://people.math.ethz.ch/~halorenz/publications/pdf/collatz.pdf).
  The primary PDF was read through its rational-cycle formula and
  mechanical-word extremal argument. It uses the shortcut clock;
  the present batch uses odd-only valuations. It provides relevant
  background, not a premise for the new elementary proof. No inspected
  statement was identified as the full height-residue Hall proposal;
  that limited check is not a novelty search certificate.
- Lagarias, [The set of rational cycles for the 3x+1
  problem](https://www.impan.pl/en/publishing-house/journals-and-series/acta-arithmetica/all/56/1/106574/the-set-of-rational-cycles-for-the-3x-1-problem),
  Acta Arithmetica56 (1990),33–53, DOI10.4064/aa-56-1-33-53.
  Publisher metadata was verified. Both attempted primary PDF routes
  returned403; the paper's contents were not verified in this batch.
  No theorem from that inaccessible text is imported or attributed.
- Hercher, [There are no Collatz m-cycles with m<=91](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.00406).
  The primary abstract page was checked. Its m counts the cycle-minimum
  parameter of that literature, not our odd period, maximum dyadic
  height B or sorted-height change count J. Earlier batch comparisons
  remain in their own reports.

## Comparison with cycle records

C008 excludes certain denominator factors only under circular order
and q<=L. It leaves q1 entirely untouched and therefore improves no
numerical Collatz cycle record, including Simons–de Weger or Hercher.
The rank progression and small-band matching are structural results
for rational cycles across d; the exact controls reject a particular
primewise extension. No new all-size q1 word class is claimed here.

The principal next target would need a different conclusion: full
ordered q1 exclusion at unbounded period and height complexity. That
target is not obtained by the present lattice theorem.
