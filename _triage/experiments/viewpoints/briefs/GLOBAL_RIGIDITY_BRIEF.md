# Goal — Establish global rigidity of finite-state Collatz invariants

**Activated 2026-10-18; full theorem delivered in V004.** This records the
accepted goal text exactly below. The earlier
[proposal](NEXT_GOAL_PROPOSAL.md) and
[partial-result brief](ARITHMETIC_REGULARITY_BRIEF.md) remain historical.
The full-classification result and completion evidence are in
[V004](../V004/GLOBAL_RIGIDITY.md) and its
[goal review](../V004/GOAL_REVIEW.md).

Prove that every finite-valued, binary-automatic coloring preserved by Collatz steps is constant, retaining the absorbing convention at 1.

Seek an argument covering arbitrary finite automata, without restricting their size or selecting a particular digit statistic.

Compare a small number of mechanisms: extending the binary–ternary kernel relations, directly constraining the automaton’s structure, and deriving a suitable generating-function identity. Pursue whichever produces a genuine implication for the full class.

Additional subclasses, finite experiments and conditional lemmas count as progress, but do not complete this goal. If bounded investigations repeatedly stall, report the theorem as unresolved and recommend a specific pivot.

Alongside the proof work, assess what the theorem would contribute toward actual Collatz convergence. Keep every missing regularity assumption explicit.

Preserve proofs, failures, controls and verification evidence in the repository. Completion requires a proof of the full classification.
