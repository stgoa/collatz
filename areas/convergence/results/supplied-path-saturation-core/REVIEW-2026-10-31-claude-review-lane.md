## Findings

1. **§3 (imprecision).** "The `2|Q|` bases `q` and `2q+1` are distinct `F`-roots" is asserted without proof. It needs: `q` is an `F`-root (`≡ 1 (mod 8)` or `≡ 3 (mod 4)`), `2q+1 ≡ 3 (mod 4)` is an `F`-root, a complementary `q` is never selected while every `2q'+1` is, and `q ↦ 2q+1` is injective. Holds.
2. **§5 (imprecision).** "arguments separated by at most 1/2" is stated without the computation; the separations are `(1 + 2^(1−s))/3` for even `s` and `(2^(1−s) − 1)/3` for odd `s`, with the bound reached only at `s = 2`. Holds.
3. **Statement 6 and §6 (imprecision).** The cutoff `N = 2^a − 1` is odd, outside the `8 | N` convention of the surrounding nodes; `U_N` and `Q_N` make sense for every `N`, so the statement stands, but the change of convention is not flagged.
4. **Hygiene (imprecision).** `check_saturation.py` verifies by `assert` only (23 asserts, no explicit exit), so under `python3 -O` it would pass vacuously. `tools/legacy_replay.py` runs it without `-O`, so the recorded PASS is valid; the migration certificate `check_saturation_recompute.py` uses explicit failure lists.
