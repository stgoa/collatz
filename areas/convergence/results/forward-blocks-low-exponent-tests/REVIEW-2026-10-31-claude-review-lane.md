## Findings

1. **Claim field and Statement item 3, the `a = 4`, `d = 4` case (imprecision, corrected).** Both read "... selected even `f` with `d ≥ 6`, or `d = 4` with `ν(f) ≢ 3 (mod 8)`", which admits nonselected `f`. For nonselected `f` at `(4,4)`, `ν(f) = f ≡ 3 (mod 4)`, so `f ≡ 7 (mod 8)` satisfies the literal condition while `B(f) = f > q` (`f − q = (115t+7)/8`): 65 such rejected inputs below `2^17`, the first `w = 1893`, `f = 10783`. The proof's (6) carries the hypothesis "`f` is selected". I inserted "selected even `f` with" in both places; the proved statement is unchanged.
2. **Section 3 (imprecision).** For selected endpoints the comparison "at the least `d`" uses the generic bound `(9n+5)/8`, monotone in `f`; the sharper table rows matter only at `(4,4)` and `(4,2)`, where they are listed. Correct as written, recorded for the reader.
3. **Certificates (imprecision).** The only certificate replays an assert-based legacy checker without `-O` (as `tools/legacy_replay.py` does); under `python3 -O` the legacy checker would pass vacuously. Disclosed in the node; recorded for consistency with the other reviews of this line.
