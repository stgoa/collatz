2. **Statement 2 (imprecision).** The rule holds on the whole progression `245 (mod 1024)`, but the new coverage is only the stated subprogression `245 (mod 3072)`: among `n = 1..59` with `n ≢ 0 (mod 3)` the twelve-rule dispatcher accepts 24 members (R41: 13, R52: 7, R46: 2, R57: 2) and leaves 16 unrecognised.
