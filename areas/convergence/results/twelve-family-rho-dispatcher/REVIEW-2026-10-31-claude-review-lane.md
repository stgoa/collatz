## Findings

1. **Statement 1 (imprecision).** The parenthesis lists the nine old rules as "R41, R44, R45, R46, R47, R48, R50, R52, R53 with its internal priority". The actual internal priority is R41 (on `E_good` only), R45, R44, R46, then R47, R48, R50, then R52, R53. Since the statement defers to the internal priority, nothing is wrong; recorded for readers.
2. **Statement 2 (imprecision).** `one_step` raises `ValueError` on an input outside `W5` (or a non-positive one) rather than returning `None`; the statement is scoped to `w ∈ W5`, where it is accurate.
3. **Certificate (imprecision).** Assert-only legacy checker, a certificate only through `tools/legacy_replay.py` without `-O`, like the other legacy replays.
