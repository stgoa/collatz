## Findings

1. Proof §2, after (5) (gap, minor). "Any weak component disjoint from 1 has divergence sum zero, so it cannot contain the sole positive source" shows that the weak component of `x` in the support graph contains 1, not yet that a directed path from `x` to 1 exists. The missing step is that every row has exactly one out-edge, so all vertices of a weak component reach the same terminal vertex, which here is the sink 1 (rows at 1 are killed); the path indicator is then the nonnegative current. The same lemma was completed in the review of [`complementary-run-bridges`](../complementary-run-bridges/README.md).
2. Statement 1 (imprecision). "The only canonical predecessor smaller than `q` is `L(q)`" is right; the exception `q = 1`, where the ray minimum `(4q−1)/3` equals `q`, is noted in the proof but not in the Statement.
3. Statement 5 (imprecision). The `O(log^5 N)` count has no explicit constant and is exercised by no certificate; only its values at the eight cutoffs are compared with enumeration (by the legacy replay, and independently here).
