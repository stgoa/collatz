2. **Section 2, row `e = 8` (imprecision).** The only row without an explicit formula ("normalize exactly; take the stated canonical forward or complete-run branch"); the implementation treats it generically through `a(Y) = 3 + v2(81U − 5) ≥ 4`, and the general prescription covers it. No change.
