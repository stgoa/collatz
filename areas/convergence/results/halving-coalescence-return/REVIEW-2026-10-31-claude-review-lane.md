## Findings

1. **§2 (imprecision).** The extraction of `T` from `T_signed` cites "a finite functional component containing 1 has 1 as its unique terminal vertex"; the reason is that a weak component of a graph with out-degree at most 1 has at most one sink, so the component of 1 is an in-tree to 1. Holds (the same lemma, with the same gap, as in R17 and R26).
2. **§3, (8)-(9) (imprecision).** The error of each progression count is strictly below 1, so (8) is strict; the bounds are loose in practice (largest observed ratio to the bound 0.12 for (8), `N = 480`, and 0.10 for (9), `N = 472`, over `N ≤ 8192`). Correct as stated.
3. **Hygiene (imprecision).** `check_halving_return.py` and `coalescence_check.py` verify by `assert` only (24 and 5 asserts, no explicit exit), so under `python3 -O` they would pass vacuously. `tools/legacy_replay.py` runs them without `-O`, so the recorded PASS is valid; the migration certificate `check_halving_recompute.py` uses explicit failure lists.
