## Findings

1. **Statement 1 and Section 1 (imprecision).** `j = v3(4^D u + 2)` is 0 exactly when `u ≡ 2 (mod 3)`; then the "two-block" word is `2^D` alone and `p = s > q`. This is half of the domain (43,693 of the 87,381 canonical `q ≡ 1 (mod 3)` below `2^20`). The text is correct as written ("exactly `j` following inverse point steps"), but a reader may expect `j ≥ 1`.
2. **Section 2, "The D=1 accepted row recovers a previously handled part of E" (imprecision).** The sentence does not say which part. For `D = 1`, `s + 1 = 2(2q+1)/3`, so `j = v3(2q+1) − 1` and `p` is R41's ancestor; the rule accepts iff `v3(2q+1) ≥ 3` iff `9 | w`. Verified: the 7,282 accepted `D = 1` inputs below `2^20` are exactly the 7,282 selected `W5` inputs with `9 | w`, all inside `E_good`; the returning row (`w ≡ 3 (mod 9)`, `j = 1`) is never accepted.
3. **Certificate (imprecision).** The replayed legacy checker verifies by `assert` and compares its receipt by `assert`; it is a certificate only through `tools/legacy_replay.py`, which runs it without `-O`. Same as the other legacy replays of this batch.
