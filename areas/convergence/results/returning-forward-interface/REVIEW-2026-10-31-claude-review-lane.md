The word "through" leaves this implicit. No change.
2. **Section 3 (imprecision).** The improved bound `(9r+1)/16` applies (5) to the seed `ν(r)` (the requests of `H_r` are those of `H_(ν(r))`); the text says only "(5) improves". Correct as used.
3. **Statement 1 (remark).** "a decidable sufficient strict-rank test" is the comparison of one rational table value with `q`; it is sufficient only, and the node says so. The unresolved inputs (102 of 228 below 32,768) remain explicit premises, as stated.
