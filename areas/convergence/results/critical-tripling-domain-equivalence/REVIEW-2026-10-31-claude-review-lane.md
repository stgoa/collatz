## Findings

1. Frontmatter `claim`, first sentence of "(Critical seeds)": "Every positive odd seed reduces to a smaller one ... unless it lies in C". The seed 1 is the absorbing state, is not in `C` and is not reduced; the body's Statement 1 correctly writes `q > 1`. Imprecision.
2. Statement 4 and section 4, row `q ≡ 2 (mod 3)`: the requested pair base is `u = (4q+1)/3 > q`, so a formal plan can request a pair input larger than the vertex it reduces; the bound (5) and "the number of requested pair inputs is logarithmic" concern the count only. The text says so ("the bound says nothing about the cost of the inputs"), but "logarithmic" could be read as a size bound. Imprecision, no change needed.
3. Controls: the node's equivalence is not a cycle-exclusion statement and cannot exclude a `3x+d` cycle; the hypothesis the controls fail is the sibling identity `S(4x+1) = S(x)` (and the halving identity), as the node states. For `3x+7` the merging analogue of (ii) holds below 4000 although `3x+7` has the cycles `{5, 11}` and `{7}`, so the implication (ii) implies (i) genuinely uses the `d = 1` identities. No error.
