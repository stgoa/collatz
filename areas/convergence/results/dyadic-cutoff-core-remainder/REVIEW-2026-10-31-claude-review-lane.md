The pattern is asserted without its reason, which is the only place where the hypothesis `b ≥ 3` enters: `3M_a` divides both cutoffs of `2^(b+6)` for every `a ≤ 6` exactly when `b ≥ 3`, so the lift counts are `≡ −t_0 (mod 3)`. The sentence is in the proof of [`disjoint-annular-recursion`](../disjoint-annular-recursion/README.md) and is supplied above.
2. Proof, "Since `⟨1, ν_(2^b)⟩ = −1`" (imprecision). Asserted without derivation; it also needs `b ≥ 3` (`n_2` even). Derivation above.
3. (8) is sharp (remark). Both bounds are attained: `−12` for every `b ≡ 5 (mod 6)` and `T ≥ 6` (first at `N = 32`, `T = 6`), and `+1` for every `b ≡ 1 (mod 6)` and `T ≥ 6` (first at `N = 128`, `T = 6`).
