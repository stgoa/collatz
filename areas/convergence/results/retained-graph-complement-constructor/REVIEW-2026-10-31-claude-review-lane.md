## Findings

1. **§2 and §4 (imprecision).** "A finite functional graph component containing 1 supplies an actual directed path to 1" needs the observation that a weak component of a graph with out-degree at most 1 has at most one sink, so the component of 1 is an in-tree; without it the component could contain a second sink above `B`. The same step makes the good outer states of §3-§4 reach 1 by directed paths, where the text only states weak connection to `[1, N]`. Supplied above; holds.
2. **§1 (imprecision).** The pushforward form of `K_N` is used without the one-line lemma `P(U_N − U_(N/4)) = 1[x ≡ 2 (mod 3)]·1[x ≤ 3N/2] + 1[x ≡ 1 (mod 3)]·1[x ≤ 3N/4]` (exactly one predecessor of each `F`-ray lies in `(N/4, N]` because `N/4` is even) that identifies it with R14's `K_N`. Verified; holds.
3. **Hygiene (imprecision).** The legacy checker `check_complement.py` verifies by `assert` only (17 asserts, no explicit exit), so under `python3 -O` it would pass vacuously. `tools/legacy_replay.py` runs it without `-O`, so the recorded PASS is valid; the migration certificate `check_profile_recompute.py` uses explicit failure lists.
