- **§1, components (3), re-derived.** `S(x) = S(y)` gives `3x+1 = 2^a z`, `3y+1 = 2^b z` with the same odd `z`; mod 3, `2^a ≡ 2^b`, so `a ≡ b (mod 2)` and `x = F^k(y)`. The inverse `F` step exists exactly at `x ≡ 5 (mod 8)` (`v₂(3x+1) ≥ 3`, against `2` at `1 mod 8` and `1` at `3 mod 4`) and lowers `v₂(3x+1)` by 2, so the F-exponent is `⌊(v₂(3x+1) − 1)/2⌋`. A selected root `p = 2^a t − 1`, `t ≡ 3^a (mod 4)`, has partner `2^(a−1) t − 1`: for `a ≥ 3` its parameter is `a − 1` and `t ≡ 3^a ≡ −3^(a−1)`, complementary; for `a = 2`, `t ≡ 1` and `2t − 1 ≡ 1 (mod 8)`. Conversely `2q + 1` has `(a, t) = (2, (q+1)/2)`, `t ≡ 1`, when `q ≡ 1 (mod 8)`, and `(a+1, t)` with `t ≡ −3^a ≡ 3^(a+1)` when `q` is complementary. A selected `p` is never a lower partner (`2p + 1` would need `t ≡ 3^(a+1) ≡ −3^a`). So every component is the F-ray of `q` joined to the F-ray of `2q + 1`, least member `q`.
- **§2, identity (1) and current (4)-(5).** `S^j(r) = 3^j 2^(a−j) t − 1` for `j ≤ a − 1` and `S^j(q) = 3^j 2^(a−1−j) t − 1` for `j ≤ a − 2` (valuation 1 while a power of 2 at least 2 remains). With `z = 3^(a−1) t ≡ 3^(2a−1) ≡ 3 (mod 4)`, `3·S^(a−2)(q) + 1 = 2(z − 1)` has valuation exactly 2, so `S^(a−1)(q) = (z−1)/2` and `S^(a−1)(r) = 2z − 1 = F((z−1)/2)`. `(I − P)(δ_x − δ_r) = δ_x − δ_r` because `S(x) = S(r)`; `D_r` telescopes; at `r = 3` with partner 1 the killed rows leave `δ_3 + δ_5`, divergence `δ_3`. Each inverse step sends `x` to `(x−1)/4 < x/4`, so `k < log₄ x`.
- **§3, quotient (6)-(9).** `q ≡ 1 (mod 8)`: `3q + 1 ≡ 4 (mod 8)`, `S(q) = (3q+1)/4`, `S(2q+1) = 3q + 2 = F(S(q))`. ## Findings

1. **§7 and Certificates, hygiene (imprecision).** [`check_normal_form.py`](check_normal_form.py) checks only by `assert`, including the comparison with the receipt. On a scratch copy whose receipt had `inverse_targets` changed to 1, `python3 check_normal_form.py` fails (exit 1) but `python3 -O check_normal_form.py` prints PASS (exit 0). [`AGENTS.md`](../../../../AGENTS.md) §6 asks certificates to hold under `-O`. The node discloses the limitation and `tools/run_certificates.py` runs without `-O`, so the recorded PASS is valid; converting the asserts to explicit exits is recommended for a later PR (the file is byte-identical legacy and was not touched).
2. **Statement 6 and §6 (imprecision).** "for odd `a ≥ 13`": the membership of `2^a − 1` in `R'_N` with coefficient 1 at `N = 12·2^(a−4)` holds for every odd `a ≥ 5` (`a = 5`: `N = 24`, `27 < 31 < 36`). The threshold corresponds to `b = a − 4 ≥ 9`, the first cutoff of the induction in [`five-ray-profile-completion-suffices`](../five-ray-profile-completion-suffices/README.md), which the node does not say. Not an error.
3. **Statement 4 (imprecision).** Only the forward direction ("every canonical predecessor is one of ...") is stated; the proof also gives the converse (every listed point is canonical and maps to `y`), which the finite enumeration `predecessors_up_to` relies on. Both hold.
