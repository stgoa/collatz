## Findings

1. (imprecision) Statement 2 says that in a failure "a unique highest critical request of rank `≥ q`, coefficient `+1`, survives". This is correct as written (unique *highest*), but it is not "exactly one request of rank `≥ q`": in 608 of 6403 failures sampled a second request has rank in `[q, n)`. The proof's sentence "every other request in (4) has strictly smaller rank than this leader" is the precise statement.
2. (imprecision) Statement 3, "the other rows are decided by 2": the rows `k ≡ 0 (mod 8)` and `k ≡ 4 (mod 16)` are in fact decided by the explicit tests of the table (zero or one transition), and only `k ≡ 2 (mod 4)` and `k ≡ 3 (mod 8)` need the general procedure. Consistent with section 4.
