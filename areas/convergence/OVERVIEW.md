# Convergence

**The question.** Does every orbit of `3x+1` reach 1? Equivalently: no orbit grows forever, and there is no nontrivial cycle.

**The approach.** Reductions of convergence to finite certificates ("packet currents"), and calibrations of known proof routes, such as Tao's almost-all theorem and positive-density results, against what they would need to prove.
