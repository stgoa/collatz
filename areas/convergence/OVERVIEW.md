# Convergence

**The question.** Does every orbit of `3x+1` reach 1? Equivalently: no orbit grows forever, and there is no nontrivial cycle. Nothing here answers it. The nodes are scoped theorems, exact computations, Collatz-equivalent reformulations and documented failures; the status of each is in [`MAP.md`](../../MAP.md).

**The approach.** Clock: odd-only, absorbing at 1 ([`conventions/NOTATION.md`](../../conventions/NOTATION.md) §1). The main programme turns convergence into the supply of **finite certificates**: signed "currents" `J` with `(I − P)J = μ` for explicit source measures `μ`, where `P` pushes mass one odd step and kills it at 1. It reformulates the conjecture several times, each time as a sharper finite obligation, and records for every failed construction the exact obstruction for the named method. Later work calibrated published routes (density theorems, predecessor counts, Tao's almost-all theorem) against what they would need to prove, always checked against the `3x+d` controls, whose actual cycles and nonabsorbing multiples of `d` refute any argument that does not use `3x+1` arithmetic ([`baseline-obligation-transfer-controls`](results/baseline-obligation-transfer-controls/README.md)).

**Who did what.** Almost all results (R1–R94 and Phase 6, 2026-10-17 to 10-18) came from the main lane (ChatGPT/Codex), starting from a ChatGPT hand-off ([`packet-currents-snapshot`](results/packet-currents-snapshot/README.md)). The Claude side lane re-checked its finite arithmetic batch by batch and contributed a few nodes of its own ([`divergent-orbit-itinerary-complexity`](results/divergent-orbit-itinerary-complexity/README.md), [`negative-cycle-backward-word`](results/negative-cycle-backward-word/README.md), [`quotient-prefix-plans-rho-decreasing`](results/quotient-prefix-plans-rho-decreasing/README.md), the experiment [`cylinder-certificates-outer-residual`](attempts/cylinder-certificates-outer-residual/README.md)). An independent Claude PR1 lane re-derived R2, R3 and R5, re-checked R4 and R6 numerically, and built the `3x+61` controls. A separate Claude audit lane rebuilt the external positive-density library (`lean/audit/`), and the Claude Lean lane (Fable) formalised its dichotomy corollary. The Claude explicit-Tao lane made the last push. Most main-lane nodes stay `written`: their "independent" reviews were the main lane's own agents, and the side lane's probes, recorded under `reviewed_by` with their scopes, usually re-checked finite arithmetic only.

## The main lines

### 1. Certificates and currents (R1–R10, PR1)

- **Packets and currents.** The stopping-work discrepancy of a signed source measure is split into zero-weight "packets"; a finite current then bounds their work at every horizon ([`finite-current-certificate-identity`](results/finite-current-certificate-identity/README.md)). Grouped from the right, depth-one packets leave a remainder on the fixed core `{5, 7, 11, 13}` with error `≤ 8 + 30⌈log₂N⌉` at every horizon ([`right-aligned-fixed-core-remainder`](results/right-aligned-fixed-core-remainder/README.md), R2, reviewed), exactly `[−12, 1]` at dyadic cutoffs ([`dyadic-cutoff-core-remainder`](results/dyadic-cutoff-core-remainder/README.md), R4). A uniform-cost version was never found ([`uniform-current-cost`](questions/uniform-current-cost.md)).
- **The criterion.** Finite currents with nonpositive residual at every cutoff are **equivalent** to Collatz ([`qualitative-current-criterion`](results/qualitative-current-criterion.md), R3, reviewed), so certificate supply is the frontier ([`packet-current-supply`](questions/packet-current-supply.md)): a reformulation, not a weakening. Five dyadic rays suffice ([`five-ray-current-criterion`](results/five-ray-current-criterion/README.md), R5, reviewed), and multiplying a cutoff by 64 adds a disjoint six-band annulus `A_N` with positive mass `99N/4` ([`disjoint-annular-recursion`](results/disjoint-annular-recursion/README.md), R6), so the target becomes currents for every annulus on 30 sequences ([`five-ray-annular-current-supply`](questions/five-ray-annular-current-supply.md)).
- **What currents detect.** Exactly the mass of each orbit component, with a complete but non-terminating search ([`orbit-component-current-criterion`](results/orbit-component-current-criterion/README.md), R7); bounded per-packet templates cannot work ([`bounded-packet-certificate-obstruction`](results/bounded-packet-certificate-obstruction.md)).
- **Partial constructions.** An explicit partial current removes `73N/48 + O(1)` of the `99N/4` positive annular mass ([`universal-annular-partial-current`](results/universal-annular-partial-current/README.md), R8); root collapse compresses an annulus to `19/22` of its norm once, not repeatably ([`annular-root-collapse-contraction`](results/annular-root-collapse-contraction.md), R9); the exposure potential `Φ` makes construction a finite integer problem, with the missing boundary flux named ([`exposure-potential-boundary-flux`](results/exposure-potential-boundary-flux/README.md), R10). The open step is that every positive plateau of `Φ` ends ([`annular-component-progress-lemma`](questions/annular-component-progress-lemma.md)).
- **Controls (PR1).** `3x+61` satisfies the criterion at every cutoff through 212 and fails at 213, because of its 41-cycle with minimum 235, so no argument using only properties `3x+61` shares can work ([`locally-collatz-like-control-barrier`](results/locally-collatz-like-control-barrier/README.md)); progress statistics do not separate them ([`progress-measures-control-3x-61`](results/progress-measures-control-3x-61.md)); the obligation splits basin by basin into "no cycles" and "no divergence" ([`class-wise-obligation-equivalence`](results/class-wise-obligation-equivalence.md)).

### 2. The residual-progress programme (R11–R94) and how it ended

- **Compilers, and why they cannot feed themselves.** A rational cone with contraction `35/27` compiles supplied packet certificates into an exact integer current, shown for `U_112` ([`finite-rational-cone-integer-certificate`](results/finite-rational-cone-integer-certificate/README.md), [`actual-graph-current-compiler`](results/actual-graph-current-compiler/README.md)); the inputs may lie above the target, so the compiler is circular. No fixed exposure depth suffices ([`unbounded-exposure-depth-necessary`](results/unbounded-exposure-depth-necessary/README.md), answering [`uniform-aggregate-exposure-depth`](questions/uniform-aggregate-exposure-depth.md)), even given the ternary order ([`fixed-ternary-order-unbounded-depth`](results/fixed-ternary-order-unbounded-depth/README.md)).
- **The outer residual.** Given absorption through `N`, an explicit profile of mass `25N/288 + O(1)` remains ([`outer-residual-strong-induction-support`](results/outer-residual-strong-induction-support/README.md), found by the side lane); completing it at every interleaved five-ray cutoff would prove Collatz by induction ([`five-ray-profile-completion-suffices`](results/five-ray-profile-completion-suffices/README.md), R24; open as [`five-ray-complementary-profile-completion`](questions/five-ray-complementary-profile-completion.md)).
- **Quotient and tripling pairs.** The sibling and halving identities give a terminating normal form `ν` and a quotient `T = ν∘S` whose termination is equivalent to Collatz ([`elementary-bridge-quotient-normal-form`](results/elementary-bridge-quotient-normal-form/README.md), R25; [`normalised-quotient-termination`](questions/normalised-quotient-termination.md)). Every root reduces to tripling pairs `P(w) = [3w] − [w]`, and universal tripling currents on a binary domain are already Collatz-equivalent ([`critical-tripling-domain-equivalence`](results/critical-tripling-domain-equivalence/README.md), R37); R40 cuts the domain to `W5 = {w ≡ 5 mod 8}` ([`critical-nine-elimination`](results/critical-nine-elimination/README.md)), the line's central open target [`universal-w5-tripling-currents`](questions/universal-w5-tripling-currents.md).
- **Rank-decreasing rules.** With the rank `ρ(w) = ν(3w)`, any finite union of strictly `ρ`-decreasing rules composes, keeping unhandled leaves ([`critical-image-rank-composition`](results/critical-image-rank-composition/README.md), R43). Rules were added domain by domain and dispatched as twelve-, fifteen- and finally seventeen-family unions ([`seventeen-family-rho-dispatcher`](results/seventeen-family-rho-dispatcher/README.md)): termination of formal conditional composition, not supply of the leaves. A strict-`ρ` recursion for all of `W5` is equivalent to a stopping-time statement for `T` ([`quotient-prefix-plans-rho-decreasing`](results/quotient-prefix-plans-rho-decreasing/README.md), reviewed; open as [`universal-w5-quotient-stopping`](questions/universal-w5-quotient-stopping.md)).
- **Barriers, each scoped to a named procedure.** No finite-label potential decreases at every run return ([`bounded-runs-exclude-finite-label-potentials`](results/bounded-runs-exclude-finite-label-potentials/README.md)); mixing a `σ` rule with a `ρ` rule produces arbitrarily long growing blocks ([`mixed-rank-growing-blocks`](results/mixed-rank-growing-blocks/README.md)) on which no potential with finitely many affine valuation centres works ([`no-finite-affine-valuation-rank`](results/no-finite-affine-valuation-rank/README.md)); a fixed survivor of the exit-18 returns needs factor complexity `liminf p(L)/L ≥ 18.285…` ([`aperiodic-exit18-return-survivors`](questions/aperiodic-exit18-return-survivors.md)), and the itinerary of any divergent orbit `liminf p(L)/L ≥ 1/log₂(3/2) = 1.7095…` ([`divergent-orbit-itinerary-complexity`](results/divergent-orbit-itinerary-complexity/README.md), reviewed).
- **The end (R75–R94).** A common critical rank basis lets `σ` and `ρ` rules share one rank ([`common-critical-rank-basis`](results/common-critical-rank-basis/README.md)). The last rounds worked on one coupled cell: computer-assisted classification of rational basins excludes every smaller positive canonical origin of the repeated target within `12r + 19` quotient steps ([`rational-basin-precision-barrier`](results/rational-basin-precision-barrier/README.md), [`fourteen-step-window-original-source`](results/fourteen-step-window-original-source/README.md)); R93 normalises the pair to the two-atom residual `[864z − 257] − [z]` ([`two-source-normalisation-first-departure`](results/two-source-normalisation-first-departure/README.md)); R94 proves that the proposed ternary child cannot be reached at uniformly bounded clocks ([`prescribed-ternary-child-unbounded-clocks`](results/prescribed-ternary-child-unbounded-clocks/README.md)). The line was **frozen at R94**, the seventeen-family dispatcher unchanged and universal supply unreached. Its final obligations: [`residual-pair-864z-257-supply`](questions/residual-pair-864z-257-supply.md) and the candidate route [`common-tail-comparison-after-r92`](questions/common-tail-comparison-after-r92.md), both feeding [`universal-w5-tripling-currents`](questions/universal-w5-tripling-currents.md).

### 3. Phase 6: route calibrations

The main lane then mapped named proof obligations (routes S: stopping, C: predecessor counting, Y: cycles, H: analytic rigidity), graded each batch as a non-transfer step, a calibration, a scoped barrier or none, and checked everything against eleven raw `3x+d` cycles and the multiples-of-`d` invariant ([`baseline-obligation-transfer-controls`](results/baseline-obligation-transfer-controls/README.md); literature scope in [`route-literature-transfer-audit`](results/route-literature-transfer-audit.md)).
- **S.** Fixed-factor stopping re-derived with explicit horizons (170 and 295 steps for `ε = 1/10, 1/100`) and a W5 pullback ([`fixed-factor-stopping-explicit-constants`](results/fixed-factor-stopping-explicit-constants/README.md), reviewed). Two scoped barriers close uniform ways of iterating it on arbitrary targets ([`arbitrary-target-linear-transport-barrier`](results/arbitrary-target-linear-transport-barrier/README.md), reviewed; [`growth-norm-transport-barrier`](results/growth-norm-transport-barrier/README.md), whose one-arrow part still contracts by `10/13`). The premise for the actual bad set stays open ([`bad-set-half-descent-transport`](questions/bad-set-half-descent-transport.md), consequences in [`bad-set-transport-bootstrap`](results/bad-set-transport-bootstrap.md)).
- **C.** The AI-generated linear predecessor-density theorem was replayed in Lean ([`lau-density-formal-replay`](results/lau-density-formal-replay/README.md), reviewed, resting on [`lau-2026-positive-density`](../../sources/lau-2026-positive-density.md)) and reconstructed in writing conditional on Tao's mixing for every `3x+d` ([`predecessor-density-conditional-reconstruction`](results/predecessor-density-conditional-reconstruction/README.md)). With it, a nonempty nonabsorbing set has positive lower density, so the remaining statistical condition is itself equivalent to convergence ([`nonabsorbing-set-linear-lower-density`](results/nonabsorbing-set-linear-lower-density/README.md), Lean dichotomy recorded; open as [`nonabsorbed-fraction-arbitrarily-small`](questions/nonabsorbed-fraction-arbitrarily-small.md) and [`unabsorbed-count-power-saving`](questions/unabsorbed-count-power-saving.md)). Two attacks on the upper side failed ([`c2a-two-level-residue-relaxation`](attempts/c2a-two-level-residue-relaxation.md), [`matched-density-gap-combination`](attempts/matched-density-gap-combination.md)).
- Phase 6 ended when the owner switched to viewpoint exploration. Every open node is Collatz-equivalent or a calibration; no step distinguishing `3x+1` from the controls was found.

### 4. The explicit-Tao barrier

A Claude lane tried to make Tao's almost-all theorem explicit: a threshold `C(δ)` below which all but logarithmic density `δ` of orbits dip, to compare with the verified range ([`explicit-tao-almost-all-constants`](attempts/explicit-tao-almost-all-constants.md); target [`explicit-almost-all-descent-threshold`](questions/explicit-almost-all-descent-threshold.md)). One round of explicit descent is proved for every odd `d` ([`explicit-valuation-large-deviations`](results/explicit-valuation-large-deviations/README.md), [`explicit-one-round-descent`](results/explicit-one-round-descent.md)). But with Tao's printed parameters the first-passage window is empty unless `log x > 6.27·10^13`, so that argument certifies no `C(δ)` below `exp(6.27·10^13)` for any `δ < 1` ([`tao-printed-window-vacuity`](results/tao-printed-window-vacuity/README.md), reviewed: a scoped barrier on the proof as printed, not on Tao's theorem). The machine-checked library's explicit mixing needs 3-adic levels `≥ 2^80000` ([`explicit-tao-via-formal-mixing-constants`](attempts/explicit-tao-via-formal-mixing-constants.md)). Whether re-optimised parameters could help is open, with a heuristic "no" ([`tao-architecture-reoptimisation`](questions/tao-architecture-reoptimisation.md)).
