## Findings

1. Imprecision (claim and Statement (a)): "`C_g` determined by the cylinder". `C_g = sum_t 6 2^(s_(a_t))` depends also on the chosen exponents `s_a` (any `s_a` with `2^(s_a) a = c (mod M)` and `u_a >= 1` works). It is determined once a choice rule is fixed, for instance the minimal exponents used by `verify_examples.py`. The inequality is unaffected.
2. Gap in a dependency's formal statement, not in this node: the Lean theorem `LeanLane.Kernel.kernel_ternary_closure` is existential (`exists a' r'`) and does not state the depth bound `a' <= a + 4` that part (b) uses; the bound is in the prose claim of [`positive-offset-kernel-ternary-closure`](../positive-offset-kernel-ternary-closure.md) and in the witnesses of the Lean proof (`a+1`, `a+2`, `a - l + 4`). Re-derived here (and checked on 6,108 sections). The `formal` label of the dependency covers less than what is used; the use is correct.
3. Imprecision (Statement (a)): the inequality `kappa_h(D) <= 1 + (C_g kappa_g(D-1))^M` holds for every invariant `h` and every positive cylinder `g`, automatic or not; "quantitatively" makes it read as a form of the automatic case. Harmless.
4. Observation (controls): the inclusion `J_D(b)` in `K^+_(1+4D)(h)` is not contradicted by `c_5` and `c_13` (above), so (b) looks `d`-uniform although the identities (2) are not; consistent with the node, which claims `d = 1` only for (a).
