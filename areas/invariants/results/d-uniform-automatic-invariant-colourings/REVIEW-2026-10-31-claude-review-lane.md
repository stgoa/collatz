## Findings

1. **Claim and Statement, the hypothesis at `n = 1`** (imprecision). `h(3n+d) = h(n)` for odd `n ≥ 1` is the raw identity including the source 1, whereas the area convention takes 1 as absorbing and imposes it only at odd `n > 1`. For `d` with `d + 3` a power of two (`d = 1, 5, 13, 29, 61, 125, 253, 509` below 1000) the two agree, since `h(3 + d) = h(2^k) = h(1)` by doubling. For other `d` it is a genuine extra hypothesis: for `d = 7` the absorbed cycle labelling (labels 1, 5, 7) has `h(10) = 5 ≠ h(1) = 1` and fails Step 2's identity at `n = 0` only. The proof uses the hypothesis exactly in `(4_d)` at `x = 1` (so Step 2 at `n = 0`) and in the Case A rewrites with `t = 1` at `N = 0` (for `d = 7`, the states `(a, r0, j1) = (1, 1, 2)`, `(2, 1, 1)` and `(a, 5, 0)`, `a ≥ 3`). The claim is correct as written, but "for `d = 1` odd `n > 1` suffices" understates the argument: odd `n > 1` suffices for every `d`. Under the absorbed convention Steps 1–4 run unchanged for odd `x ≥ 3` (`S0` grows by at most one) and give `h(x) = q̄(x mod g)` there; for `2^k ≡ d (mod 3)` with `2^k > d + 3`, `n_k = (2^k − d)/3` is odd, `> 1`, never a multiple of `d`, and `≡ 2^k·3^(−1) (mod g)`, in the `⟨2, 3⟩`-orbit of 1, so `h(1) = h(2^k) = h(n_k) = q̄(1)`. (Consistently, the absorbed labelling of `3x+7` is not a function of `n mod 7` and has 263 sections by depth 9.) Not a correction; recorded for a later strengthening.
2. **Step 1, Case B** (imprecision). "`ℓ = v2(r0) < a`" is correct but vacuous: `r0` is odd by the choice of states, so `ℓ = 0`, `s = a` and `U = 2^a N + u` always.
3. **Step 1, the window** (gap in exposition only). The two ingredients are given but not the resulting window; `S ≥ 1 + (3/2)⌈d/12⌉` closes (`S ≤ 3` for `d = 5`, `S ≤ 10` for `d = 61`, against the minimal 1 and 8 found by the probe and by me). One line completes it; the claim is unaffected.
4. **Evidence paths and links** (typo, repaired). After the move into a folder the two evidence paths and seven body links needed one more `../`; applied, no text changed.
5. **Checks (e)** (imprecision). "States with `a ≤ 7`, 31 consecutive `n` each" describes the comparison on the labellings; the script also checks the integer relations for `a ≤ 12`. Consistent with the receipt; no change.
