## Findings

1. Statement (YAML claim and body): "there is a threshold, independent of `u, v, k, ℓ`, above which `den(w) ≥ 2^((η − ε)·A)`" does not say what is thresholded. The proof's (T2) is: there is `A_0(ε)` such that every word of the class with total `A ≥ A_0` satisfies the bound. Severity: imprecision (meaning unchanged; recorded, not edited).
2. `UNEQUAL_PROBE.md` §5.3, ineffectivity of Theorem 2. The only non-elementary step of the proportional branch is (11), that is the factor `E/U_0^e = 1 − (3^b_0/2^a_0)^e`. Without it, (8)–(10) give the exact all-size inequality `den(w) > (1/2)·(1 − (V_0/U_0)^e)·2^(η·A)` for every proportional pair with `K ≠ 0` and `k, ℓ ≥ 2` (verified on the 1,169,800 cases above). With EXT-1 ([`musson-collatz-gap-all`](../../../../sources/musson-collatz-gap-all.md)) the factor is at least `2^(−172)·(b_0e)^(−341)`, so that branch admits an effective threshold. "Ineffective as proved here" is accurate, but the ineffectivity is not intrinsic to the proportional branch. Severity: imprecision (an observation; no change to the claim).
3. `UNEQUAL_PROBE.md` §3 uses `H` for the height maximum and §5.2–5.3 reuse `H` for `gcd(Rk, Sℓ)`, while §5.1 uses `L, h` in the formal node's sense and §5.3 uses `L, H` for the reduced counts. Severity: typo-level notation.
4. README Proof summary: "All imported estimates, local terms and exceptional sets are explicit in the proof" holds for the local terms and the exceptional translates of Input B; the finite exceptional set of Input A is not and cannot be explicit. Severity: imprecision.

## Verdict

HOLDS. Conditional, as stated, on the two Corvaja–Zannier inputs; the threshold is ineffective. Certificate passed. No correction applied.
