# Review of multi-rotation-four-mismatch-exclusion

Independent review of the paused attempt (legacy S10). Scope: hypothetical nontrivial cycles of the raw odd-only `3x+1` map in a continuous model of S7's aligned single-drop structure; the node claims model-level findings and no exclusion. The question for an attempt is whether "Why it fails" and "What survives" are correct and claim nothing unproved.

## What I checked

- Read `AGENTS.md`, `conventions/SCHEMA.md`, `conventions/NOTATION.md` §3, `CONTRIBUTING.md` ("Review a node"), `areas/cycles/OVERVIEW.md`, the node, its dependencies [`three-mismatch-exclusion`](../../results/three-mismatch-exclusion/README.md) (claim) and [`four-mismatch-sign-dichotomy`](../../results/four-mismatch-sign-dichotomy.md), the end-witness law [`paired-state-run-law`](../../results/paired-state-run-law.md), the S8 note [`four-mismatch-size-method-limit`](../four-mismatch-size-method-limit/README.md), and the claims of [`short-rotation-mismatch-growth`](../../results/short-rotation-mismatch-growth/README.md) (`c_4 = 0.0752`) and [`largest-letter-rho-reduction`](../../results/largest-letter-rho-reduction.md) (`0.0220·m` for `s ≤ 4`) quoted in "What survives": both match.
- **Model definitions, re-derived.** Along the chain `x, x+h, x+2h, …` the letters are `V`, then `v₂` (`a` times), `v₃` (`b`), `v₄` (`c`). For the rotation `kh` with `k ≤ min(a, b, c)` the mismatches are: `X` (`V` against `v₂`), `X − kh` (`v₄` against `V`), `P₁ − jh` and `P₂ − jh` for `0 ≤ j < k` (`v₂`/`v₃` and `v₃`/`v₄`), and `X − jh` for `1 ≤ j < k` (`v₄` against `v₂`) exactly when `v₂ ≠ v₄`: `2 + 2k + (k−1)[v₂ ≠ v₄]` mismatches, six for `2h` when `v₂ = v₄`, as S8 says. Constraint: the run law gives `max(n_e, n_(e+kh)) > 2·3^t` at the end `e` of a run of `t` agreeing positions; each odd step multiplies a state by at most `(3/2)(1 + q/3n)`, so `y_p ≤ σ·p + o(p)` from the minimum, and with `ℓ'/m → 0` the law becomes `t ≤ r·max(pos(e), pos(e + kh))`, `r = σ/log₂3 = 0.36907`. The envelope bounds the real profile from above, so the model's constraints are necessary conditions, in the limit, for any word with this chain structure and its minimum right after `V`. The model adds beyond the theorem exactly what the node lists: the single-chain structure, the position of the minimum, `ℓ'/m → 0` (finite `m` needs the margin to beat `342·log₂m + 172`), and only the end-witness inequalities.
- **Single rotation, by hand** (`v₂ = v₄`): `p₂ ∈ (0, 1−h)`, `p₁ ∈ (1−h, 1)` is feasible iff `h ∈ [(1−r)², 1 − (1−r)²] = [0.398, 0.602]` (S8's window); both inner points in `(1−h, 1)` iff `h ∈ [1−r, 1 − (1−r)³] = [0.631, 0.749]`; both in `(0, 1−h)` iff `h ∈ [(1−r)³, r] = [0.251, 0.369]`. My numeric scan (`h/500`, `p/200`) finds exactly these three windows.
- **The `v₂ = v₄` exclusion by `h` and `2h`, by hand** in S8's configuration with `h < 1/2`: for the rotation `2h` every mismatch `e ≥ 1 − 2h` has witness `e` itself (`e + 2h − 1 < e`). `p₁ − h` and `p₁` are both `2h`-mismatches; with no other mismatch between them the run has length `h` and witness `p₁`, so `h ≤ r·p₁ < 0.369 < 0.398`, a contradiction; hence `p₂` or `p₂ − h + 1` must lie in `(p₁ − h, p₁)`. The ratio chain `u_(i+1) ≤ u_i/(1−r)` from `Q = 1 − 2h` to the last point `≥ 1 − r` forces `1 − 2h ≥ (1−r)^5`, i.e. `h ≤ 0.450`.
- **Independent implementation** (my own script from the node's description). Coarse grid: 179,700 `h`-feasible points, best `(h, 2h)` margin `−0.02194` at `(0.6525, 0.55, 0.865)`, identical to the receipt; finer grid (`h` step 0.001, `p` step 0.002): `−0.02008`; random local search: `−0.0097` at `(0.3334, 0.5435, 0.2102)`, as the node's random search. **Exact linear programmes:** within a cell (fixed point order, wraps and witness choices) every constraint is linear in `(h, p₁, p₂)`; 96 cells found from 600,000 random samples plus a grid; every LP optimum is negative; the supremum of the `(h, 2h)` margin for `v₂ = v₄` is `−0.009667`, at `h = 1/3`, `(p₁, p₂) = (0.2103, 0.5436)`. The exclusion therefore holds in the model with a uniform margin `0.0097m`.

## Findings

1. **Imprecision (§3 table, §6 (b)).** The uniform margin of the `v₂ = v₄` exclusion is the continuous supremum `0.0097m` (my LPs; the node's own random search gave `−0.0097`), not the coarse-grid value `0.0219m` that §6 (b) uses; the finite-`m` threshold is then `m₀ ≈ 7.0·10^5` rather than `≈ 2.9·10^5`, still below `2^21`. The claim says "grid margin", so no claim is wrong.
2. **Imprecision (§3 table).** "`h ∈ [0.2575, 0.7425]`" is the hull of three windows with infeasible gaps `(0.369, 0.398)` and `(0.602, 0.631)`.
3. **Imprecision (§3, discrete check).** "Rotations `3h`, `4h`, `5h`, `7h` and `9h` fail too" holds at `ℓ = 71` and `100`; at `ℓ = ℓ*` only `2h`, `3h` and `5h` fail.
4. **Imprecision (§3, later updates).** The discrete words fail by 20,261 to 42,780 bits ("20,000–42,000").
5. **Imprecision (§5, third bullet).** The symbol `d` in "`|h|_m < d`" is undefined (not the `3x+d` parameter; presumably the descent-window length).
6. **Caveat (§5 "Net" and headline).** "The method yields at most `ρ ≥ 5`" is a model-level inference: `s = 5` surviving the end-witness relaxation with the envelope shows that this relaxation cannot exclude `s = 5`; no discrete `s = 5` word meeting all of S7's inequalities (start witnesses, ones-run law) was exhibited. The node labels everything model-level and claims no exclusion, so this is a caveat, not an error.

## Verdict

HOLDS. The model's definitions are correct necessary conditions in the stated limit, every exclusion computation reproduces, the central one is confirmed by exact linear programmes, and "Why it fails" and "What survives" claim nothing unproved. Findings 1–6 are imprecisions recorded under History; no text changed.
