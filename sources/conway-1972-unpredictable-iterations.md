# Conway (1972): generalized Collatz iterations are undecidable

## Citation

Conway, *Unpredictable iterations* (1972). The bibliographic details are completed from general knowledge, not verified against the source.

## What we use

The statement as commonly reported: for maps `g(n) = a_r·n + b_r` on `n ≡ r (mod m)` (rational `a_r`, `b_r` making `g` integer-valued), there is no algorithm that, given such a map and a starting value, decides whether the orbit reaches 1. The map is part of the input; nothing is claimed for the fixed `3x+1` map. Used only as context in the [decidability overview](../areas/decidability/OVERVIEW.md) and the question [`undecidable-collatz-family-threshold`](../areas/decidability/questions/undecidable-collatz-family-threshold.md).
