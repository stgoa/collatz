# Languasco–Luca–Moree–Togbe: rational form of Matveev's bound

## Citation

Languasco, Luca, Moree and Togbe, [*Sequences of integers generated by two fixed primes*, Theorem 2.1](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12188-025-00293-9). It states the positive rational "product of rational powers minus one" version of Matveev's theorem ([matveev-2000-linear-forms](matveev-2000-linear-forms.md)). ## What we use

Bases `2, 3`, exponents `−A, m`, field degree one; `D_w = 2^A − 3^m > 0` makes the form nonzero. For `B ≥ max(A, m)`:

```
log(1 − 3^m/2^A) > −K_0·(1 + log B),
K_0 = 1.4·30^5·2^(9/2)·log 2·log 3 < 600,000,000          (natural logarithms)
```

With `A < 2m` (forced for a nontrivial integer cycle since every state is at least 3) and `B = 2m`:

```
log(1 − 3^m/2^A) > −600,000,000·(1 + log(2m)).
```

The rational estimate `K_0 < 598,752,000 < 600,000,000` is checked locally by C011's `check_exclusion_constants.py`; the analytic theorem itself is imported.

Legacy results using it: C011 (two-defect exclusion and bounded-denominator finiteness), C013 (effective bounded-denominator finiteness and the denominator-one complexity restriction). S06 and S11 replace it with EXT-1. The C013 audit rechecked the source's definition of Λ and Theorem 2.1, and found that the side feed's suggested extra `log 2` loss does not apply to this product-minus-one form.
