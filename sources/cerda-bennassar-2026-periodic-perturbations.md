# Cerdá Bennassar (2026): perturbations of the periodic word (1,2)

## Citation

Miguel Cerdá Bennassar, *Perturbaciones de la palabra (1, 2)^q
en los ciclos de Collatz: reducción general y obstrucciones
aritméticas al cierre*, [version 2](https://zenodo.org/records/23412904),
16 October 2026.

## What we use

Prior-art comparison only. Theorem 8, printed page 7, gives
fixed-modification-count finiteness using an integrality
and power-chain argument with exponent `log_8(9)`.
Theorem 10, printed page 10, states that increasing at most
19 distinct positions of `(1,2)^q` cannot produce a positive
integer cycle, except the all-2 word. Both phases and
arbitrary positive increments are covered.

This subsumes the numerical four-increase corollary of
[`expanding-background-defect-growth`](../areas/cycles/results/expanding-background-defect-growth/README.md)
at the statement level. No theorem here is imported as
an assumption of the repository's independent proof.
