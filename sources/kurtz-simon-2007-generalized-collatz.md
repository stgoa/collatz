# Kurtz–Simon (2007): the generalized Collatz problem is Π⁰₂-complete

## Citation

Kurtz and Simon, TAMC 2007 (LNCS 4484). Details completed from general knowledge, not verified against the source.

## What we use

The statement as commonly reported: the problem "given a generalized Collatz map of Conway's form, does every positive integer eventually reach 1?" is `Π⁰₂`-complete. This is the class-wide universal version of Conway's single-orbit result. It concerns a family in which the map is the input; it says nothing about whether the fixed `3x+1` statement is provable or true. Context only.
