# Monks–Monks–Monks–Monks (2013): strongly sufficient sets

## Citation

Keenan, Kenneth G., Kenneth M. and Maria Monks, *Strongly sufficient sets and the distribution of arithmetic sequences in the 3x+1 graph*, [arXiv:1204.3904v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1204.3904v2) ([author copy](https://www.mathematicalgemstones.com/maria/papers/mmmm.pdf)). Year 2013 as given in the legacy literature list; venue not stated (citation incomplete in the legacy record). Shortcut clock.

## What we use

- **Theorem 4.1:** each positive target coprime to 3 has a backward path into every prescribed residue class (with modulus-dependent bounds on the number of odd inverse branches, not on total shortcut length). Used in V003 and in V004's optional progression finish (`automaton_route.md` checks the positive-tail and ternary-unit conditions explicitly).
- **Corollary 5.8, Theorem 6.4:** every shortcut orbit contains a state `≡ 2 (mod 9)`; every divergent orbit and nontrivial cycle contains `≡ 20 (mod 27)`. Comparison only in the residual-progress sufficient-sets audit.
