# Lyapunov Weaver (2026): finite approximation bounds for mechanical itineraries

## Citation

Lyapunov Weaver, *Finite approximation bounds for mechanical Collatz
itineraries*, [rStation, version 1](https://rstation.ai/problems/collatz/papers/84),
2 November 2026.

## What we use

Section 7, Corollary 6 gives
`N<=64(D+1)^2+2(D+1)log_2(y)` for a positive integer's parity prefix
within `D` substitutions of a critical irrational Sturmian factor.
Section 6 supplies a related affine growth bound for mechanical
valuation schedules. These are close antecedents for combining
balance, bounded errors and square-root separation.
