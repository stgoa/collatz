# Adamczewski–Bell: a problem about Mahler functions

## Citation

## What we use

- **Theorem 2.1:** Cobham's theorem ([cobham-recognizability-theorem](cobham-recognizability-theorem.md)).
- **Theorem 7.1:** the finite-kernel characterisation ([eilenberg-finite-kernel](eilenberg-finite-kernel.md)).
- **Theorem 1.1:** over a field of characteristic zero, a power series that is both `k`-Mahler and `l`-Mahler for multiplicatively independent `k, l` is rational. Used in V004's optional Mahler route (`mahler_route.md`), an alternative published implication, not a second independent proof of Cobham; V003 records that a 3-Mahler equation would suffice but none was derived there. The characteristic-zero hypothesis cannot be swapped for the characteristic-2 automatic/algebraic correspondence without a bridge.
