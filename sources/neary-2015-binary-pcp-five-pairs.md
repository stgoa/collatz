# Neary (2015): the Post correspondence problem is undecidable for five pairs of binary words

## Citation

Neary, STACS 2015. Details completed from general knowledge, not verified against the source.

## What we use

The Post correspondence problem (given pairs `(u_1, v_1), …, (u_k, v_k)` of words, is there a nonempty index sequence with `u_(i_1)⋯u_(i_t) = v_(i_1)⋯v_(i_t)`?) is undecidable already for five pairs over a two-letter alphabet. Only "undecidable for binary words" is needed by [`synchronised-coding-pcp-reduction`](../areas/decidability/results/synchronised-coding-pcp-reduction/README.md); Post's original 1946 theorem with a binary encoding would also do.
