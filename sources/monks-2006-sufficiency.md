# Monks (2006): sufficiency of arithmetic progressions

## Citation

K. M. Monks, *The sufficiency of arithmetic progressions for the 3x+1 Conjecture*, Proc. AMS 134 (2006), 2861–2872 ([author copy](https://monks.scranton.edu/files/pubs/SufficiencyRev4.pdf)); Theorem 1.1, Lemmas 2.1–2.4 (backtracing, attributed there to Wirsching) and Lemma 3.8. Shortcut clock.

## What we use

**Theorem 1.1:** every nonconstant infinite arithmetic progression is sufficient: every positive orbit merges with the orbit of some member of the progression. Lemma 3.8 constructs a merger in prescribed congruence classes.

Legacy use: V003 (an invariant coloring constant on one infinite arithmetic progression is constant everywhere; so a monochromatic dyadic cylinder argument rederives a consequence of established sufficiency), V004's optional progression finish (not needed by its central proof). The residual-progress sufficient-sets audit uses it as a precedent and scope comparison only: it gives no bound placing the merger witness in a finite certified range.
