## What we use

Scope comparison only. The manuscript claims a
`log(n)*log(log(n))` lower order for fixed-dictionary
cycle complexity, using Subspace Theorem inputs. Thus
[`expanding-background-defect-growth`](../areas/cycles/results/expanding-background-defect-growth/README.md)
does not claim an asymptotic record: its scope is one
expanding background with explicit elementary constants
under EXT-1.
