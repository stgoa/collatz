# Simons (2008): bounds for cycles of generalized Syracuse sequences

## Citation

John L. Simons, *On the (non-)existence of m-cycles for
generalized Syracuse sequences*, Acta Arithmetica 131.3
(2008), 217-254. [Publisher page](https://www.impan.pl/en/publishing-house/journals-and-series/acta-arithmetica/all/131/33/82891/on-the-non-existence-of-m-cycles-for-generalized-syracuse-sequences),
[journal PDF](https://www.impan.pl/shop/en/publication/transaction/download/product/82891).

## What we use

Methodological comparison for
[`expanding-background-defect-growth`](../areas/cycles/results/expanding-background-defect-growth/README.md).
Section 2.2, steps 2(a)-(c), uses integrality to lower-bound
the product of local minima, chains their magnitudes by a
power inequality, and obtains an exponentially small drift
upper bound at fixed circuit count. Section 4.2 applies
the strategy to generalized `px+q` sequences.

The repository result rederives its shifted-coordinate
argument for copies of a fixed expanding valuation word.
No numerical bound or exclusion from this paper is imported.
The paper's local-minimum count is not the repository
result's count of modified blocks, and these clocks must
not be identified.
